OLP Homework Hub: Online Assignments, Exams and Learning Resources

OLP Homework Hub is an online learning platform for teachers and students. Teachers can organise classes, prepare assignments and examinations, share educational resources and support classroom discussions. Students can access learning activities and submit their work online.

Sign in to OLP · Create an account

What can you do with OLP Homework Hub?

Online assignments and examinations

Teachers prepare classwork and assessments, collect submissions and review marks before returning results to students.

Learning resources and classroom discussions

Find academic materials in the resource centre and continue class discussions beyond the school day.

AI-assisted marking with teacher review

Where enabled, teachers can use marking keys to assist assessment review. Teachers remain responsible for checking and approving final marks.

For teachers and students

OLP brings classroom organisation, homework, examinations and learning resources into one connected workspace. Private classwork and student submissions require sign-in.

Frequently asked questions

What is OLP Homework Hub?

It is a digital platform for organising and completing school learning activities online.

Can students use OLP on a phone?

OLP Homework Hub is designed for use on mobile phones and desktop browsers.

Are student answers public on Google?

No. Student work and class dashboards are protected by sign-in and are not intended for public search results.

Connected OLP learning platforms
